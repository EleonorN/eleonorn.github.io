SEO for an equestrian e-commerce brand

Keyword research and on-page optimisation for a product page selling elastic training bands for horses.

Role SEO
Tools Ahrefs, SEO Pro Extension, Google Sheets
Context Digital Marketing, Berghs
Year 2025

The challenge

A Swedish equestrian e-commerce brand wanted to improve the organic visibility of a product page for elastic training bands for horses, with the goal of reaching more potential customers and increasing sales.

The challenge was to identify the most relevant keywords and understand how the existing product page could be optimised to rank higher in search results.

My approach

I researched relevant keywords in Ahrefs and analysed search volume and intent to understand how potential customers search for the product and the problems it solves.

For the relevant keywords, I also looked at the Google search results to see whether product pages, category pages or informational content were ranking.

I then audited the existing product page to identify opportunities to improve its on-page SEO.

The insight

Most relevant searches were informational and focused on problems such as rehabilitation, groundwork and strengthening a horse's back. Commercial searches for the product itself existed, but at considerably lower volumes.

The product page also relied heavily on the brand's own product terminology rather than the language potential customers were using in search.

The recommendation

Capture existing demand: optimise the product page around the keyword "träningsband för häst", including the title, meta description and H1.

Build future demand: create useful content around higher-volume informational searches to reach horse owners while they are still researching their problem. By introducing the product as a relevant solution within that content, the brand can build awareness earlier in the customer journey and stay top of mind when the customer is ready to buy.
